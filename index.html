Xuelian Cao (曹雪莲)

I am a postdoctoral researcher in the WingTecher Lab at the School of Software, Tsinghua University, supervised by Prof. Yu Jiang. I received my Ph.D. in Computer Science and Technology from Southwest University in 2025, advised by Prof. Zhiming Liu. From September 2024 to February 2025, I was a visiting student at iTrust, Singapore University of Technology and Design (SUTD), supervised by Prof. Jianying Zhou.

My research focuses on designing provably secure and efficient cryptographic constructions, particularly lightweight authentication schemes and privacy-preserving schemes, and on the security analysis of cryptographic implementations.

I can be reached at xl-cao@mail.tsinghua.edu.cn.
